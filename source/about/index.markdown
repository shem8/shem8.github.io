My name is Shem Magnezi.

I'm a software engineer with more than a decade of experience across client and server-side systems, real-time architectures, big data, mobile, and web. I care a lot about growth mindset and about helping engineering teams work well together.

After senior IC roles at WeWork and Meta, I was the CTO and co-founder of [Wilco](https://trywilco.com), a platform that helps developers level up through hands-on practice. Today I'm a Senior Staff Engineer at Lemonade.

I also like to [speak at conferences](/talks) and to build [side projects](/projects).

You can find me at the beach with a beer in my hand, or at a Maccabi Tel-Aviv football game.
