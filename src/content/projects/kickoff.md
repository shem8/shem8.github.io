A fan’s home for World Cup 2026 resale-ticket data. A Chrome extension: fans browse FIFA’s resale site as usual and each browser sends back what it sees, so there is no central scraper to block. It covered 104 matches and tracked 8.1M seat changes for $0 in scraping costs, and market-update posts on Reddit reached 455K views and brought almost 1,000 users. Covered by WIRED and The Times.
