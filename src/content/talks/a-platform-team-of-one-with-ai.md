AI can help you build a side project quickly. The harder question is whether you can operate it without becoming its permanent support team. Drawing from my experiments building and running multiple projects alongside a full-time job, I describe the problem as a platform-engineering problem at a team size of one: reducing repeated work, making delegation usable, and deciding which tasks deserve human attention.
