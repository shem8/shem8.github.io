Many times, you and your team will decide to choose a path that you know might come back to haunt you. The same happened to me as the CTO of Wilco, when I chose a fast but fragile solution to one of our early problems. That solution later cost us $10k, a significant amount for a small startup.

In this session I share that horror story, along with the tools you have as a technical leader to assess the risks, mitigate problems when they show up, and balance long-term and short-term solutions.

Presented at DevOpsDays Tel Aviv 2024 (Israel). I also wrote about it for [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/when-losing-10000-to-cryptojacking-is-a-good-investment/).
