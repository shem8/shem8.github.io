About

My name is Shem Magnezi.

I’m a software engineer with more than 20 years of experience across client and server-side systems, real-time architectures, big data, mobile, and web. I work heavily with AI: I help teams adopt it, accelerate the work of every department, and build tools, products and agents with it. I care a lot about growth mindset and about helping engineering teams work well together.

I’ve been part of two exits. I was the first employee at MyRoll, which was acquired by AVG, and the CTO and co-founder of Wilco, a platform that helps developers level up through hands-on practice, which was acquired by Lemonade. I’ve also held senior IC roles at WeWork and Meta. Today I’m a Senior Staff Software Engineer at Lemonade. The details are on my career page.

I also like to speak at conferences and to build side projects.

You can find me at the beach with a beer in my hand, or at a Maccabi Tel-Aviv football game.
